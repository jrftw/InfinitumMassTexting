A clear allowance

Free includes 25 live-send credits per calendar month, one campaign and one variation. Dry runs and safety protections are free. Credits reset at midnight UTC on the first day of each month; your account shows that time in your local time zone.

A live-send authorization consumes one credit, including a test message. Failed or uncertain attempts may count because Messages cannot prove delivery. Campaigns reserve enough credits before starting so the app can warn you before a partial send. Unused reservations are released after stopping or expire after two idle hours.

Signing out or reinstalling does not reset your allowance. The licensing service stores account and usage records, not your recipients or message text.
