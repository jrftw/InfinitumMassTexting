Privacy Policy

PRIVACY POLICY Infinitum Mass Texting — Effective September 20, 2026 Infinitum Imagery LLC Policy version 2026-09-20.2 What the app stores The app stores contact names, phone numbers, optional email/social usernames/notes, consent/source records, imported text, message variations, personalized messages, settings, do-not-contact numbers, campaign status, error logs, local acceptance state and timestamps on your Mac. The three campaign slots share this Mac’s contact library, suppression list and available recipient history; there is no cross-device synchronization by Infinitum. Its data folder is ~/Library/Application Support/InfinitumMassTexting/. These files are not separately encrypted by the app; they use local file permissions. Your device encryption and backup settings apply. What leaves your Mac When you explicitly confirm a live campaign, the selected recipient number and message are passed to Apple Messages for transmission using your configured account and, where applicable, your carrier. Apple, carriers and recipients handle this information under their own policies. Apple Messages syncing and your device backups are controlled by your account and OS settings. Automatic update checks contact api.github.com on launch, periodically and when a live campaign needs a fresh version check. GitHub receives normal network information, such as IP address, request time, and HTTP headers. The request identifies the app’s public release repository. It does not include your contacts, message bodies, opt-out list or campaign logs. Downloads are hosted by GitHub. The public website is hosted on Google Firebase Hosting, with a GitHub Pages mirror. Firebase Hosting processes visitor IP addresses for abuse detection and usage analysis (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). Google’s privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy. The website adds no advertising, analytics SDK, tracking pixels, contact form or optional tracking cookies. Hosting providers may process normal connection and security logs under their own policies. GitHub’s privacy statement is available at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. No app telemetry or AI service The app has no advertising SDK, cloud contact storage or AI-service integration. It does not upload campaigns to Infinitum. Message-quality hints run locally and do not determine whether content was generated by AI. The app does not read incoming conversations to detect opt-outs. Accounts, subscriptions and usage Firebase Authentication processes your email, sign-in credentials and account verification. Refresh credentials are stored in macOS Keychain; passwords are not stored by Infinitum’s app. The account website uses session storage for sign-in tokens, cleared on sign-out or when the browser session ends. Do not share your sign-in. Infinitum’s Firebase backend stores your account ID/email, plan, complimentary grants, random device/campaign/attempt identifiers, monthly credit counts, reservations, timestamps and administrative audit records. These records enforce shared allowances and do not contain recipient names, phone numbers, message bodies or your local contact library. The app needs an online account authorization before each live-send attempt. Dry runs use no credits. Stripe processes payment and subscription information under its privacy policy (https://stripe.com/privacy). Payment details are entered in Stripe-hosted checkout; Infinitum’s app never receives full card details. Our backend retains Stripe customer/subscription identifiers and access status. Complimentary accounts need no payment method. We use this information to provide access, prevent misuse, support customers and maintain appropriate billing records.
