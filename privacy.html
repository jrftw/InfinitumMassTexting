Privacy

PRIVACY POLICY Infinitum Mass Texting — Effective September 18, 2026 Infinitum Imagery LLC What the app stores The app stores contact names and phone numbers, imported text, message variations, personalized messages, settings, do-not-contact numbers, campaign status, error logs, local acceptance state and timestamps on your Mac. Its data folder is ~/Library/Application Support/InfinitumMassTexting/. These files are not separately encrypted by the app; they use local file permissions. Your device encryption and backup settings apply. What leaves your Mac When you explicitly confirm a live campaign, the selected recipient number and message are passed to Apple Messages for transmission using your configured account and, where applicable, your carrier. Apple, carriers and recipients handle this information under their own policies. Apple Messages syncing and your device backups are controlled by your account and OS settings. Automatic update checks contact api.github.com on launch, periodically and when a live campaign needs a fresh version check. GitHub receives normal network information, such as IP address, request time, and HTTP headers. The request identifies the app’s public release repository. It does not include your contacts, message bodies, opt-out list or campaign logs. Downloads and the public website are also hosted by GitHub. GitHub’s privacy statement is available at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. No app telemetry or AI service The app has no analytics, advertising SDK, app account, cloud contact storage or AI-service integration. It does not upload campaigns to Infinitum. Message-quality hints run locally and do not determine whether content was generated by AI. The app does not read incoming conversations to detect opt-outs. Your controls and retention Local campaign records remain until you erase them. Clear Temporary Cache keeps contacts, campaigns, logs and opt-outs. Erase All Local App Data removes app-managed records and preferences after confirmation. Uninstalling the app alone does not remove Application Support data. Erasure does not delete Messages conversations, exported files, browser downloads, device backups or information already received by others. Previously contacted numbers are no longer remembered after erasure. Support and public contributions Information you voluntarily post to the public GitHub repository is public. Never include personal contacts, message contents, passwords or unredacted logs. Ask for a private contact channel before providing sensitive support information. Infinitum uses information voluntarily provided for responding to support requests and operating the product; information may be retained as needed for that purpose or legal obligations. Your responsibilities If you manage creators’ information for an organization, you are responsible for having permission to process it and providing any required notices. This app is not designed for use by children under 13. Contact and changes For privacy questions or to request a private contact channel, use https://github.com/jrftw/InfinitumMassTexting/issues without including sensitive details. We will date material policy changes in the app and on the distribution page. This policy does not remove rights provided by applicable law.
