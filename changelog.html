What’s new.

1.0.8 · Build 2

Your columns. Your messages.

Every named CSV column can personalize a message: use {username}, {time}, {order_number} or another imported heading. Spaces become underscores. Custom fields survive saved contacts and campaign preparation, with complete message previews. A time field inserts text; it does not schedule sending.

Clear Recipient List removes names and numbers only from the editable draft while retaining templates, saved contacts and history. Fixed the bundled build number to match the release.

Free, Pro, Business, Scale and Unlimited plans are available. Paid plans support scheduling, three campaigns and multiple variations. Every plan keeps the same safety controls and provider restrictions.

79 Mac tests passed. Developer ID signed and Apple-notarized for download outside the App Store. Follow the installation guide.

1.0.8 · Build 1

Save your words. Check your deliveries.

New local Templates section with up to 10 variations, search, editing, duplication and campaign loading. Copy a variation as written, or use Copy with Name for a personalized message you can paste and send yourself.

New delivery-review panel with the exact message and attempt time, Open Messages, Copy Phone Number, manual status records and official SMS retry instructions. Original send results are retained. The app cannot automatically detect every delivery failure and does not resend uncertain messages.

Mac sign-in stays in Keychain and account details refresh automatically. Website sign-in persists across browser restarts. Clear Cache preserves templates; explicit local-data erasure removes them.

73 Mac tests, 35 backend tests and seven browser sign-in tests passed. Install or update using this guide.

1.0.7 · Build 1

Clearer progress. More reliable controls.

The entire Progress screen scrolls, with readable recipient cards and wrapping error details. Fixed opt-outs during account authorization leaving campaigns stuck or test sends incorrectly marked complete. Added Ackvio in Settings, a referral-program shortcut and clearer Apple/carrier guidance.

The referral website offers 5% of the first eligible subscription payment, a 30-day hold and a $50 eligible-balance minimum. Payouts require owner review. Free and Pro remain the available plans; higher paid tiers are not live yet.

66 Mac tests and 35 backend tests passed. Universal Intel and Apple silicon build, Developer ID signed and Apple-notarized. Follow the installation guide and finish or pause your campaign before replacing the app.

1.0.6 · Build 1

Your workspace, with a plan.

Verified accounts, 25 Free live-send credits each calendar month, Pro subscriptions through Stripe, and an owner dashboard for complimentary access. Pro adds 2,500 monthly credits, scheduling, three active campaigns and multiple variations. Dry runs, safety tools and local exports remain available on every plan. Contacts and message content stay on your Mac; account and usage totals are stored securely online.

After updating, create and verify your account on the website, then sign in through Account & Plan in the app. Existing campaigns remain local and recover paused.

1.0.5 · Build 1

Send on your schedule.

Choose a start date and time for each campaign using your Mac’s detected time zone. Review the exact time before confirming. Keep the app open and Mac awake with its lid open. Pause and Stop cancel a waiting start; restarting always requires manual Resume. Starts missed by more than five minutes pause for review. Dry Run still sends nothing.
