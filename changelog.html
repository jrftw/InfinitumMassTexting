What’s new.

1.0.6 · Build 1

Your workspace, with a plan.

Verified accounts, 25 Free live-send credits each calendar month, Pro subscriptions through Stripe, and an owner dashboard for complimentary access. Pro adds 2,500 monthly credits, scheduling, three active campaigns and multiple variations. Dry runs, safety tools and local exports remain available on every plan. Contacts and message content stay on your Mac; account and usage totals are stored securely online.

After updating, create and verify your account on the website, then sign in through Account & Plan in the app. Existing campaigns remain local and recover paused.

1.0.5 · Build 1

Send on your schedule.

Choose a start date and time for each campaign using your Mac’s detected time zone. Review the exact time before confirming. Keep the app open and Mac awake with its lid open. Pause and Stop cancel a waiting start; restarting always requires manual Resume. Starts missed by more than five minutes pause for review. Dry Run still sends nothing.

1.0.4 · Build 1

Room for three.

Three separate campaign workspaces with independent preparation, review and progress. One ordered Messages sending queue, shared contact history and opt-outs, plus Pause All and Stop All. Every unfinished campaign recovers paused.

1.0.3 · Clearer safeguards

Fixed the confirmation/readiness loop. Persistent approval, connection checks, full preflight reports, suspicious-number review, opt-outs, history, test variations and optional staged launches.

1.0.2 · Contacts, your way

Optional searchable contact library, editable details, CSV/text paste and one-time recipients.

1.0.1 · A clearer workflow

Dry Run and Live guidance, preparation countdown, sender setup, progress improvements and version/commit display.

1.0.0 · Hello, Mac

Native macOS messaging campaigns, reviews, local logs, onboarding and direct distribution.
