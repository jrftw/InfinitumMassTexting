GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers before you download.

Does this send three messages at exactly the same time?

No. Up to three campaigns run independently, but share a queue that sends one recipient at a time through Messages. Each campaign has its own delays and controls.

Which phone number sends the messages?

The app uses the enabled Messages account available on your Mac. Set your sending identity in Messages settings and verify it with a test. The app does not rotate numbers or spoof identities.

Is my contact list uploaded to Firebase?

No. Firebase hosts this website and handles verified accounts, plan access and usage totals. Your contacts, messages, opt-outs and campaign logs stay on your Mac. Live messages go through Apple Messages and your carrier where applicable. Update checks and downloads use GitHub.

Can I use it without creating a contact library?

Yes. Paste a list or enter one phone number for a one-time campaign. Saving contacts is optional.

Will it avoid spam filtering or guarantee delivery?

No. Use expected, authorized communications and honor opt-outs. The app helps prevent mistakes; Apple and carriers control delivery and account restrictions. A sent status means Messages accepted the command.

How do I install or update it?

Download the Mac ZIP, double-click it, and drag the app to Applications. Quit the old app before replacing it. See the step-by-step guide.
