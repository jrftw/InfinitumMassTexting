Control before every campaign.

Prepare with confidence

Paste CSV or text for a one-time campaign, or use an optional searchable contact library. Add, edit and remove contacts with only a phone number required. Preview invalid and duplicate numbers before sending.

Make it personal

Write your own message variations. Use {name} for each creator’s name and review the exact text.

Stay in control

Start with a dry run. Choose batches and delays, pause safely, resume pending contacts, and keep local logs.

From contact list to reviewed campaign.

  1. Open Messages and confirm that your account can send normally.
  2. Add a contact as Alex, +14155550101, one per line. Use +country code for phone numbers.
  3. Write a message such as Hi {name}, here’s our creator update.
  4. Review and run a simulation. Dry run never sends a message.
  5. When ready, choose Progress → Archive & Reset, turn Dry Run OFF in Prepare, then Review Campaign → Confirm & Send Live.

Use only recipients you are authorized to contact. Maintain opt-outs in the do-not-contact list. Apple and carrier rules still apply.

Your campaign data stays on your Mac.

Contacts, text, progress and logs are stored locally. The app has no analytics or cloud campaign account. GitHub receives normal connection information during automatic update checks; contacts and message contents are never included. Live messages are transmitted by Apple Messages and applicable carriers.

Install in a few steps.

Download only InfinitumMassTexting-macOS.zip from the latest release. The CSV template and checksum are optional. GitHub’s “Source code” files are not the app.

  1. Finish or pause your campaign, then quit Infinitum.
  2. Double-click the downloaded ZIP in Downloads.
  3. Drag InfinitumMassTexting.app to Applications. Choose Replace when updating.
  4. Open the app from Applications. Your saved data stays on your Mac; unfinished campaigns remain paused.
  5. Choose Progress → Check Connection, then Resume Campaign when ready.

Questions, answered.

Is this an iPhone app?

No. This is a native macOS app for macOS 14 and later. SMS availability depends on your Mac/iPhone setup.

Can staff use it?

Authorized staff can use official copies under the proprietary license. Each Mac keeps its own campaign history and do-not-contact list; there is no shared team database.

Does it guarantee delivery or avoid spam detection?

No. Sent means Messages accepted the command. It does not prove delivery. The app does not bypass platform controls, switch sender numbers, or provide AI-detection evasion.

How do updates work?

The app checks the official GitHub releases automatically. Live sending requires a verified current version. Download and replace the app to update; local campaigns remain available.

Can I copy or rebuild the source?

No source license is granted. Public downloads are proprietary binaries. The Software License allows authorized use and necessary installation/backup copies, with exceptions required by applicable law.
